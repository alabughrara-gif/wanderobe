    FAQ

    Good questions

    What if something doesn't fit?

    Every booking includes a backup size of the main piece. If neither works, message your stylist. They'll do their best to swap it the same day, and if they can't, you'll get a refund for that look.

    How does hotel delivery work?

    Your stylist coordinates with your hotel's front desk or concierge and drops off a labelled garment bag the day before your first event. On checkout day, leave it at the front desk for pickup.

    What if I spill wine on it?

    Normal wear and minor spills are covered by cleaning. Optional damage protection covers bigger accidents. Without it, repair or replacement costs come out of your deposit.

    Who are the stylists?

    Independent local stylists, boutique owners and fashion lovers who own their inventory. Each one is vetted before they can accept bookings.

    I'm not staying at a hotel. Can you deliver to an Airbnb?

    Yes. Short-term rentals within our service area are fine, and we'll arrange a delivery window with you.
